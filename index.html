Reviewed a live clinical product used across 150+ NHS sites and ranked 30+ usability issues by clinical impact.

  • Ran contextual inquiry with clinical teams at West Hertfordshire Teaching Hospitals, which revealed a redundant data-entry step.
  • Led its removal. The change saves nurses and practitioners up to 4 hours of admin a day and was adopted UK-wide.
  • Delivered annotated, developer-ready redesigns to senior leadership, plus UX assessment guidelines and a plan for a scalable UX research programme.
